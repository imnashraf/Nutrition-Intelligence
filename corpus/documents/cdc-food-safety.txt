## For people at increased risk
[Risk Factors](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/risk-factors/index.html)
[Safer Food for Children Under 5 Years Old](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/children-under-5.html)
- [Safer Food for Pregnant Women](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/pregnant-women.html)
Pregnant women are more likely to get sick from certain germs spread through food, like Listeria.


- [Safer Food for Adults 65 Years and Older](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/adults-65-older.html)
As people age, their risk of getting food poisoning increases.


- [Safer Food for People with Weakened Immune Systems](https://www.cdc.gov/food-safety/foods/weakened-immune-systems.html)
Having a weakened immune system may make it harder to fight foodborne germs and sickness.
